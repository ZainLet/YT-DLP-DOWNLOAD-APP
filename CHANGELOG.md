# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-29

- Native macOS application with a dark translucent window and Liquid Glass controls.
- Batch queue with two concurrent downloads, cancellation and completed-file access.
- Expanded video/audio containers, resolution, FPS, source codecs and audio quality.
- Real source-format inspection and manual yt-dlp selectors.
- Standalone Apple Silicon distribution with yt-dlp, FFmpeg, FFprobe and Deno.
- PKG installer, DMG, ZIP, checksums and matching third-party sources.
- Portuguese and English documentation.

Primeira release pública. A interface está em português; os READMEs estão em português e inglês. Histórico e fila são mantidos apenas durante a sessão. App com assinatura ad hoc, sem notarização Apple.
